
# 👥 Customer Segmentation using K-Means

## 1. Introduction

Customer segmentation is a technique used to divide customers into groups based on similar characteristics. In this project, K-Means clustering is used to segment customers based on their income and spending behavior. This helps businesses understand different customer groups and target them effectively.

---

## 2. Abstract

This project applies the K-Means clustering algorithm to identify distinct customer segments. By analyzing features such as annual income and spending score, customers are grouped into clusters. These clusters help businesses in making data-driven decisions for marketing and customer engagement.

## 5. Key Insights

* Customers are grouped into distinct segments based on behavior
* Identified high-income high-spending customers
* Found low-income low-spending customer group
* Recognized potential target customers
* Helps in personalized marketing strategies

---

## 6. Conclusion

K-Means clustering effectively segments customers into meaningful groups. These insights can help businesses improve customer targeting, increase sales, and enhance customer satisfaction.
